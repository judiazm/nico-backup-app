Privacy policy

Nico backup is a private tool used only by its owner. It uses Google Drive access to upload and delete the owner's own encrypted backup files in the owner's own Drive.

  • It does not collect, sell or share any data with anyone.
  • Google account tokens stay on the owner's own computers.
  • No analytics, cookies or tracking.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/judiazm/nico-backup-app.
